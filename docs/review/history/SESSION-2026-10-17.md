## Owner rules restated 17 Oct

- Nothing is suppressed: a Dependabot PR, failing run, warning, issue or agent finding is applied,
  fixed, or kept open with a written reason. Never closed or ignored to make a page green.
- "Impossible" is not a finding. Record what was tried, keep the item open, try the next angle
  (an up that should be a down is still a fix).
- Agents never merge; the owner's controller merges after gates.
