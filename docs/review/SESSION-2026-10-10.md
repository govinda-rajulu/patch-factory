## Packet W13 (one pull request)

1. **Lost patches dropped by name** (`src/build/lost_patches.py`, build.sh step 5b): before inputs are
   captured, every include name the bundle does not offer (or offers only for other app versions)
   is removed in the runner's checkout, named in the log (`PATCH_DROPPED`) and in the release notes.
   After patching, a requested name the patcher did not apply is accepted the same way. More than
   max(3, a quarter) lost, or `strict_patches: true`, fails the build. Tests: `lost_patches_contracts.py`.
2. **Provider fallback** (`src/build/build_attempts.py`, Manual Patch step `Patch apk`): attempt 1 is
   the old build; after a failure, the next untried candidate (other primaries, then
   `fallback: true` ones) is built pinned, with its `overrides` (version, version code, dpi, extra
   bundles...) over the target, from a clean checkout, without the daily Plan's prepared inputs.
   At most 2 more attempts. Manual Patch input `provider` builds one candidate on purpose.
   `resolve.sh` and `poll.sh` skip fallback candidates. Preflight checks every candidate's
   effective target. Tests: `build_attempts_contracts.py`.
3. **Release notes**: optional "Built with fallback provider ..." and "Built without N chosen
   patch(es) ..." lines (summary keys `fallback`, `dropped`).
4. **10. Selection watch** (`selection-watch.yml`, 07:41 IST daily, `src/etc/selection_watch.py`):
   removes chosen names a provider stopped offering (cap as above; exclude lists never edited),
   re-seeds provider name baselines, commits to main through the main-writer queue and pauses
   while any `packet/*` branch exists; lists new patches waiting for the owner with the
   "5. Add target" inputs; digest of every provider's releases plus `MorpheApp/morphe-patches`
   (state on the `watch-state` branch); per-app "better provider?" line from the community index;
   one standing issue "Selection watch". Tests: `selection_watch_contracts.py`.
5. Records: `onboarding/instagram.md` (new), `onboarding/facebook.md` (De-Vanced fallback, Share
